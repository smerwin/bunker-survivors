Bunker Survivors: automated playtest

40 headless games, 416 waves. Five bot commanders per mode: balanced, marine-only, mech (tanks first), random, and passive (never buys anything).

Findings

  1. The horde never gets big. Peak zerg on screen tops out around 26 in lane and 15 to 17 in the arena, against caps of 60 and 90. The cause is a spawn-pacing bug: each wave's groups are spaced by spread / remaining groups, so a 100-unit wave takes about 100 seconds to trickle in during a 30-second wave. By wave 19 the queue holds 193 zerg while only 80 die. Fixing it (even spacing across the wave) would make late waves a real flood.
  2. Lane is a slow burn, then a wall. The balanced bot survives to wave 19 to 21, about 10 minutes. The bunker is barely scratched until wave 10, then damage climbs steadily. No sudden-death cliffs were seen.
  3. The 360 arena is too short. Runs end at wave 5 to 10, about 3 minutes, and siege tanks and SCVs get overrun because zerg come from every side. Marines-only does best (wave 9); mech does worst (wave 5.5).
  4. Minerals pile up with nothing to buy. In lane, everything worth buying is owned by wave 10. Unspent minerals average 1,500 and reach 7,000 by the end. That's the clearest fun problem: after wave 10 there are no decisions left.
  5. Build choice matters too little. A bot buying at random (wave 18) nearly matches the tuned build (wave 20), while marine-only and mech builds trail. Doing nothing dies at wave 5 in lane and wave 2 to 3 in the arena, so early purchases matter.
  6. Downtime is low. Only 3% of wave time has no zerg on the field, so pacing between waves is fine.

Suggested tuning

  • Fix spawn pacing, then re-tune the wave budget so peak horde reaches 40 to 60 in lane by wave 15.
  • Add repeatable mineral sinks: more tank slots, extra bunkers or turrets, armour levels, and an ultimate such as an orbital strike or a Battlecruiser.
  • Arena: give the bunker 360-degree range or a second bunker, and stretch waves so runs last 8 to 12 minutes.
